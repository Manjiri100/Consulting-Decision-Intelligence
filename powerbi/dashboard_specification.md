# Power BI Dashboard Specification

## Executive Performance
KPI cards: Enterprise Performance Index, KPIs Below Benchmark, Material Gaps, Average Data Quality and High-Priority Interventions.

## Benchmarking
Business unit vs enterprise benchmark, quartile comparison, regional performance and drill-through.

## Root Cause
Performance gap by event type, estimated impact, severity and business-unit drill-through.

## Data Trust
Data-quality trend, records below threshold and KPI trust status.

## Decision Intelligence
Immediate Intervention, Management Review and Monitor queues with supporting evidence.
